P6

Proliferative hnRNP-Driven mRNA Splicing/Processing Program

This program reflects a proliferative, TP53-repressed state in which hnRNP family members (HNRNPA2B1, HNRNPA1, HNRNPC, HNRNPU) and chromatin/nucleosome factors (HMGB1, HMGN2) drive mRNA splicing and processing to support endothelial cell growth and glycolytic metabolism.
