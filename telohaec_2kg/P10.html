P10

Mitochondrial OXPHOS and Cytoplasmic Translation

This program couples mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation (MT-CO1, MT-ND4, MT-CYB) with cytoplasmic protein synthesis (RPL7, EEF1A1), and is driven by mitochondrial RNA/translation regulators LRPPRC and TFAM, reflecting coordinated energy production and biosynthetic capacity in endothelial cells.
