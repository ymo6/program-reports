P3

Mitochondrial OXPHOS and Cytoplasmic Ribosome Biogenesis

This program couples mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation genes (MT-CO1, MT-CYB, MT-ND4) with cytoplasmic ribosomal proteins (RPL37A, RPS4X) and translation machinery, reflecting coordinated energy metabolism and protein synthesis capacity, regulated by LRPPRC and TP53 in endothelial cells.
