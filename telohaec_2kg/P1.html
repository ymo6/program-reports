ER Chaperone-Mediated Proteostasis and Survival

This program reflects an ER chaperone/unfolded-protein-response network (HSPA5, HSP90B1, PDIA3) coupled to translational control (EEF1A1, CAPRIN1) that protects aortic endothelial cells from apoptosis under proteotoxic stress.

PDIA3
supported

PDIA3 is a known ER chaperone whose loss worsens ER stress and apoptosis under proteotoxic stress, in hepatocytes and T cells, not endothelial cells.

  • PDIA3 Knockdown Exacerbates Free Fatty Acid-Induced Hepatocyte Steatosis and Apoptosis
    PDIA3 knockdown increases ER stress markers (GRP78, p-PERK) and apoptosis via PERK-CHOP pathway under FFA stress in hepatocytes.
    2015 · PMID 26214517
  • PDIA3 orchestrates effector T cell program by serving as a chaperone to facilitate the non-canonical nuclear import of STAT1 and PKM2
    PDIA3 described as ER chaperone ensuring glycoprotein folding/quality control via calnexin/calreticulin, supporting proteostasis role.
    2024 · PMID 38822524

HSP90B1
mixed

HSP90B1/gp96 is confirmed as an ER-resident chaperone for client proteins, but evidence only covers TLR folding in immune cells, not endothelial proteotoxic survival.

  • Toll-like receptor chaperone HSP90B1 and the immune response to Mycobacteria
    HSP90B1 (gp96) is an ER chaperone required for folding/surface expression of TLRs and integrins in macrophages. HSP90B1 knockdown in monocyte-like cells did not alter TLR2 expression or Mtb replication, showing no role in that specific survival/defense pathway.
    2018 · PMID 30550567
