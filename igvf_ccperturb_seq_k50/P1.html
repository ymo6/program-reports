HUSH Complex Retroelement and lncRNA Silencing

This program marks heterochromatic loci (lncRNAs, pseudogenes and retroelement-proximal transcripts such as MALAT1 and DLEU1) normally silenced by the HUSH complex (TASOR, RESF1, SETDB1), which become derepressed as hPSC-derived mesoderm transitions toward artery endothelium, peaking at day 2.
